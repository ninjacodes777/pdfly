pdfly merge, split, extract and inspect PDFs - right in your browser

Pick two or more PDFs. They are merged in the order shown - use the arrows to reorder.
Pick one PDF and enter page ranges like 1-3,4-6 or single pages like 1,2,3. You get one new PDF per range.
Your browser may ask to allow multiple downloads - please allow it to get every part.
Pick one PDF and enter the pages to keep, like 2,4-6. Pages come out in the order you list them.
Pick a PDF to see its page count, size and metadata.

Everything runs on your device - your PDFs never leave your phone. CLI version and source: github.com/Zezo998/pdfly
